Every deal is run through a solver first, and only one with a proven winning line is dealt. Losing then means the line you chose, not the shuffle. Finding one takes a moment, and it rules out the hardest deals — the solver has to see the win to allow the deal.

How to play

Build the four foundations up from Ace to King by suit. Stack tableau cards down in alternating colours. Only Kings go into an empty column. Drag a card, or tap it and then tap where it should go. Stuck? Hint points out a move — press it again for another one. If the deal ever runs out of plays, the game says so and ends.
